# Herbie

**Your gig manager, inside Claude.**

Herbie is a free Claude skill for working musicians: any genre, any instrument, any level. Ask it *"any updates on my gigs?"* and it reads your email and calendar, then tells you what needs doing, like a manager texting you.

Written by a working musician, for working musicians.

## What Herbie does

- **Event updates.** Finds everything about your concerts, gigs, sessions, deps and auditions in your email: offers, contracts, logistics, payments, changes. It sorts them by what to do first, with one line on the problem and one line on the next step.
- **Dates that work.** When a venue offers a window, Herbie checks your calendar and suggests 2 or 3 dates, each with a reason.
- **Replies in your voice.** Drafts answers to venues, promoters and fixers that sound like you.

Herbie never sends, accepts, declines or signs anything. You approve every step.

## Install

1. Download **herbie.zip** from the [latest release](../../releases/latest).
2. In Claude, add it as a custom skill: click **"+"**, then **"Create skill"**, then **"Upload a skill"**, and upload the zip.
3. Connect your email (Gmail or Outlook) and calendar (Google or Outlook) to Claude. Calendly is optional.

Skills need code execution turned on in Claude. Full instructions: [Use skills in Claude](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/12512180-use-skills-in-claude).

Then just ask: *"Any updates on my gigs?"*

## Good to know

- Herbie only sees the accounts you connect, and only runs when you ask.
- It manages events already coming in. It doesn't find new gigs, negotiate fees or give legal advice.
- Emails are treated as information, never as instructions.
